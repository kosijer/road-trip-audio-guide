Pitch-style voiceover for the **Bring Your Own Project** demo video (max 2 minutes).  
Target runtime: **~1:45–2:00**. Ad energy, FOMO first, then solution; AI + Slingshot near the close.

Audience: Publicis Sapient Engineering Exchange judges (creativity, innovation, engineering passion). Slides/UI detail can live in the separate PowerPoint.

## English (submit version)

### 0:00 – 0:25 · Hook

You just drove past a castle.  
Past a battlefield.  
Past the one place that would’ve made the whole trip.

And you never knew.  
Because you were driving.  
Because nothing said: *turn. this matters.*

That’s not a quiet road.  
That’s a **missed road.**

### 0:25 – 0:55 · Problem

From A to B, navigation does its job: fastest. Shortest. Straight ahead.

But everyday apps **don’t pitch the stops along the way.**  
They don’t whisper what’s to your right.  
They don’t know what *you* care about.  
No personalization. No “this one — right now.”

So the landscape stays silent.  
And the FOMO hits when you’re already home.

### 0:55 – 1:25 · Solution

**Along** isn’t navigation.  
Along is the guide that rides with you.

Maps keeps the route.  
Along says: *Something you’d love is ahead. You’ve got a minute to decide.*

Walking a new city? One tap — what’s around you, spoken.  
Driving? The story arrives in time — not after you’ve passed it.  
And the more you use it, the better it learns *your* taste.

Not a list. Not a screen. **A voice.**

### 1:25 – 1:50 · AI + Slingshot

Under the hood: AI picks what’s worth your attention and writes the story like a real guide — not a Wikipedia dump.

And **Slingshot** — our Sapient workflow engine — orchestrates the chain: weather, place, details, narration, packaged result.  
Not a slideware demo. A live step inside a real product.

### 1:50 – 2:00 · Close

A to B doesn’t have to mean: forget everything in between.

**Along.**  
Hear what you’re missing — while you can still turn.

### Voiceover script

**0:00 – 0:20 · The gap in normal navigation**

Here’s a normal trip in Google Maps: A to B, turn-by-turn, job done.

What you don’t get is the interesting stuff *between* those pins — a castle off the route, a battlefield worth two minutes, something matched to *your* taste. Navigation stays silent about the journey.

**0:20 – 0:35 · Meet Along**

That’s what **Along** is for.

Along isn’t another navigator. Maps keeps the route. Along is the spoken companion beside it — personalized places, in time to decide.

**0:35 – 0:55 · Around**

Walking or stopped: open **Around**, pick a mood, tap **Around me**.

Along finds what’s nearby, filters with context like weather and hours when it can, and speaks a short story. Need more options? **More nearby** lists extras without talking over you.

**0:55 – 1:25 · Drive**

Driving: start a trip. While you’re moving, Along looks *ahead* on your heading — not behind you.

First you get a **Coming up** teaser: what it is, roughly when you’ll reach it, and Skip if you don’t care. Stay with it, and you get the full narration before you’ve already passed.

That’s the stop Maps never pitched.

**1:25 – 1:40 · History & taste**

Every story lands in **History**. Thumbs up or down feed a taste model, so the next suggestions skew toward what you actually like — not a generic tourist list.

**1:40 – 1:55 · Under the hood (short)**

Under the hood: POIs from open knowledge sources, optional Cloudflare **Clef** to pick what matters, **Gemini** to write the spoken script, on-device TTS to play it.

For Around, we can also run narration through **Sapient Slingshot** — a real workflow: weather, pick, details, narration, packaged result — inside the app, not a slide.

**1:55 – 2:00 · Close**

Along: hear what’s along the way — while you can still turn.
