# Along — BYOP demo narration

Pitch-style voiceover for the **Bring Your Own Project** demo video (max 2 minutes).  
Target runtime: **~1:45–2:00**. Ad energy, FOMO first, then solution; AI + Slingshot near the close.

Audience: Publicis Sapient Engineering Exchange judges (creativity, innovation, engineering passion). Slides/UI detail can live in the separate PowerPoint.

---

## English (submit version)

### 0:00 – 0:25 · Hook

You just drove past a castle.  
Past a battlefield.  
Past the one place that would’ve made the whole trip.

And you never knew.  
Because you were driving.  
Because nothing said: *turn. this matters.*

That’s not a quiet road.  
That’s a **missed road.**

### 0:25 – 0:55 · Problem

From A to B, navigation does its job: fastest. Shortest. Straight ahead.

But everyday apps **don’t pitch the stops along the way.**  
They don’t whisper what’s to your right.  
They don’t know what *you* care about.  
No personalization. No “this one — right now.”

So the landscape stays silent.  
And the FOMO hits when you’re already home.

### 0:55 – 1:25 · Solution

**Along** isn’t navigation.  
Along is the guide that rides with you.

Maps keeps the route.  
Along says: *Something you’d love is ahead. You’ve got a minute to decide.*

Walking a new city? One tap — what’s around you, spoken.  
Driving? The story arrives in time — not after you’ve passed it.  
And the more you use it, the better it learns *your* taste.

Not a list. Not a screen. **A voice.**

### 1:25 – 1:50 · AI + Slingshot

Under the hood: AI picks what’s worth your attention and writes the story like a real guide — not a Wikipedia dump.

And **Slingshot** — our Sapient workflow engine — orchestrates the chain: weather, place, details, narration, packaged result.  
Not a slideware demo. A live step inside a real product.

### 1:50 – 2:00 · Close

A to B doesn’t have to mean: forget everything in between.

**Along.**  
Hear what you’re missing — while you can still turn.

## Serbian (pitch draft)

### 0:00 – 0:25 · Hook (FOMO)

Prošli ste pored zamka.  
Pored bitke.  
Pored mesta zbog kog bi se drugi okrenuli.

A vi? Ništa. Jer ste vozili. Jer nije bilo vremena. Jer niko nije rekao: *stani — ovo vredi.*

To nije loš put. To je **propušten put.**

### 0:25 – 0:55 · Problem

Od A do B, navigacija radi svoj posao: najkraće, najbrže, pravo napred.

Ali uobičajene aplikacije **ne predlažu usputne tačke**. Ne kažu šta je desno od vas. Ne pitaju šta *vama* znači. Nema personalizacije. Nema „baš ovo, baš sada“.

Zato pejzaž ostaje nem.  
A FOMO raste tek kad stignete kući.

### 0:55 – 1:25 · Rešenje

**Along** nije navigacija. Along je vodič koji vozi *sa vama*.

Dok Maps drži kurs, Along šapuće:  
*Ispred je nešto što bi ti voleo. Imaš minut da odlučiš.*

Šetaš novim gradom? Jedan tap — i čuješ šta je oko tebe.  
Voziš? Priča stiže na vreme, ne posle.  
A što duže koristiš — to bolje pogađa *tvoj* ukus.

Ne lista. Ne ekran. **Glas.**

### 1:25 – 1:50 · AI + Slingshot

Ispod toga radi AI: bira šta je vredno, piše priču kao živ vodič.

A **Slingshot** spaja ceo lanac — vreme, mesto, detalji, naracija — u jedan pametan workflow.  
Ne demo. Pravi korak u aplikaciji.

### 1:50 – 2:00 · Close

Od A do B više ne mora da znači: sve između zaboravi.

Along.  
Čuj šta propuštaš — dok još možeš da skreneš.
